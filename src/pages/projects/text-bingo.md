Back then, I only know "Bingo" as a game where we write numbers 1-25, shuffled. After that, every player will take turn guessing the numbers and when a player hits the first 5 marked block (vertical, horizontal, or diagonal), the player wins.

Nowadays, a bingo can be used for a prediction, e.g. to predict how a story will unfold inside a game. In the example below, I used the example of my Final Fantasy XIV: Evercold, which will launch next year. It's a bit too far still, and I may or may not change the predictions! Let's see.
